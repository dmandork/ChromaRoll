HOW TO TEST — Shop buy crash (Black Pouch / 6th slot)

Drop these over your ChromaRoll folder:

  states/shop.py
  screens.py
  tests/test_shop_drag_and_menu.py
  HOW_TO_TEST_SHOP_BUY.txt

The crash
  Clicking Buy used shop_charms[i] from a rect list that could be longer than
  the stock (Gambler's Grimoire appended an extra Buy onto buy_rects; a
  double-click before redraw did the same). IndexError on shop_charms[i].

What changed
  - Buy maps 1:1 onto shop tiles. Out-of-range clicks are ignored.
  - Clicking the charm tile (not just the tiny Buy button) purchases it.
  - Full row (6/6 on Black, 5/5 otherwise) says "No charm slots left!"
    instead of the fake "Not enough coins!"
  - Grimoire free rune is claimed by clicking the rune tile, not a leftover Buy.

Fast check
  1. Unlock All → New Game → Black Pouch.
  2. Beat a blind, open shop, buy a charm. Should equip, no crash.
  3. Fill all 6 slots, click another Buy → "No charm slots left!", charm stays.
  4. Double-click a shop charm → one purchase, no crash.
  5. Headless: python tests/test_shop_drag_and_menu.py
