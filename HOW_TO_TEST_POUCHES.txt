------------------------------------------------
The four gated pouches
------------------------------------------------
Red / Blue / Yellow / Green still start unlocked.

  Ghost Pouch   EASY     Add 10 dice from shop packs (lifetime).
  Black Pouch   MEDIUM   Beat the Stake 2 boss (must play 6 blinds).
  Plasma Pouch  HARD     Beat the Stake 4 boss.
  Erratic Pouch HARD     Win a run (Stake 8 boss).

Locked tiles on the pouch carousel are muted with a LOCKED badge.
Hover shows the quest (Ghost also shows 3/10 style progress).
Clicking a locked pouch toasts the quest — it does not start a run.

DEBUG Achievements → Unlock All still grants every pouch.
Reset clears them again.

If you already beat Stake 2 / 4 / won a run before this drop, the matching
pouch(es) unlock on the next shop / score / win event (catch-up). Ghost
starts at 0 pack-dice because that stat is new.

------------------------------------------------
Ghost identity
------------------------------------------------
+1 starting Glass die (already worked).

Now also:
  - Special dice packs (the Gold/Silver/Glass/Rainbow pack) are 4× more
    likely to show in the shop row.
  - Standard color-dice packs mix specials into the offer.

Add a die from any dice pack → counts toward Haunting (10).

------------------------------------------------
Plasma identity
------------------------------------------------
Mix bonus, not average-and-square. Held colors (Rainbow ignored for the count):

  5 colors  +120 chips
  4 colors  +80
  3 colors  +40
  2 colors  0
  1 color   −40 (mono tax)
  All Rainbow  +120 (full spectrum)

No ×1.5 blinds. Modifier line: "Plasma mix +80" or "Plasma mono -40".

------------------------------------------------
Fast checks (DEBUG on)
------------------------------------------------
1. New Game. First row: Red Blue Yellow Green. Arrow: Black Ghost
   Erratic Plasma all LOCKED. Click Black → toast "Beat the Stake 2 boss."
2. Achievements → Unlock All. Back. New Game. All eight pouches clickable.
3. Pick Ghost. Shop: special dice pack should show up more often. Buy a
   standard dice pack — Gold/Silver/Glass/Rainbow can appear in the offer.
4. Reset achievements. Buy 10 dice packs across runs (or one long run).
   Toast "Haunting — Ghost Pouch". New Game → Ghost is open.
5. Challenges → Stake 2 → Jump to Boss → Force Win. Black unlocks.
   Stake 4 Force Win → Plasma. Stake 8 Force Win → Erratic + Life Milestone.
6. Unlock All, New Game, pick Plasma. Blind targets match a Red pouch.
   Hold 5 different colors → "Plasma mix +120" and a bigger total.
   Hold five of one color → "Plasma mono -40" and a smaller total.
7. Mid-run save/load on Ghost or Plasma: shop weighting / mix bonus
   still apply.
