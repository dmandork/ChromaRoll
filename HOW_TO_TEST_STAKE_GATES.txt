HOW TO TEST — Intensify gate + boss pools
=========================================

Drop over your ChromaRoll folder:

  data.py
  ChromaRoll.py
  screens.py
  states/blinds.py
  tests/test_boss_stakes.py
  ChromaRoll_Game_Features.md

Headless:  python tests/test_boss_stakes.py

In game
-------
Stake 1 blinds screen:
  Intensify is gray, caption "Unlocks at Stake 2". Click does nothing.
  Boss card is an Easy effect (Reroll Ration / Discard Drought / Penalty / Cap / Score Dip / Coin Freeze).
  Boss card shows EASY in green.

DBG → jump to Stake 2:
  Intensify is live ("optional D20 boon").
  Boss still Easy.

Jump to Stake 3–4: Medium names appear (Hold Limit, Color Fade, …). Card shows MEDIUM.
Jump to Stake 5–6: Hard names appear (Hold Ban, Charm Eclipse, …). Card shows HARD.
Jump to Stake 7–8: no Easy bosses.
Endless (stake 9+ via play): Hard only.

Debug "Select Boss" still lists every boss so you can force-test.
